A photographic proposal · Filario Hotel & Residences · Lake Como

The Same Water

One family, two shores: the sea and the lake.

In May I photographed Hotel Windsor in Laigueglia. The series became «The Same Sea, the Same Letters» — the hotel, the beach, and a hundred and fifty years of letters written from that shore. This page is a proposal for its second chapter: the same eye, carried from the family's sea to the family's lake.

01

The idea

Windsor and Filario already mirror each other — the four-hands dinners between Savò and Filo, the «Lago Mediterraneo» idea. A photographic series sea ↔ lake gives an image to what the family already practises at the table. Even the hotel's name carries the thread: Filario — filo + Lario, the silk thread of Como stretched between two shores of one story.

The mirror has its drama. Windsor is the historic house — three names, archive postcards, the nineteenth century still in the walls. Filario is the young house. And where the sea's archive is postcards, the lake's archive is letters: Pliny, Stendhal, Liszt and Shelley all wrote theirs from these shores. The axis of the Windsor chapter — people have been writing the same letters from this shore for a hundred and fifty years — continues on Como literally. Here the letters are two thousand years old.

02

The visual language

Quiet colour: hard light, architecture as protagonist, colour held in planes — a deliberate contrast to the archival, nostalgic register of the Windsor chapter. The mirror is not in repeating the device, but in repeating the gaze.

  • Morning — the hotel still in the mountain's shadow, the lake glass-still, mist over the water: the quiet register, the inverse of Windsor's bright mornings.
  • Noon — hard overhead light: the geometry of the façade, the texture of Ceppo di Gré stone, the waterline of the infinity pool dissolving into the lake.
  • Evening — the western sun crossing the lake straight into the façade: warm stone against blue water; the building steps forward as the protagonist.

The colour signature stays Filario's own — blue, green, grey, with one dusty-pink accent per frame. No more than one.

03

The letters of the lake

«That one I call Tragedy, this one Comedy: the first stands as if on the high boots of the stage, the second on light sandals».

— Pliny the Younger · on his two villas on Lake Como · 1st century

«When you write the story of two happy lovers, set them on the shores of Lake Como».

— Franz Liszt · letter from Bellagio · 1837

«This lake exceeds anything I ever beheld in beauty».

— Percy Bysshe Shelley · letter · 20 April 1818

Two villas, two moods of one shore — Pliny's frame is two thousand years old, and it is the frame of this diptych: the sea and the lake as two moods of one family.

04

Practicalities

  • Time on site — two nights, three days: two dawns and two sunsets. The Windsor chapter was made in exactly two nights as well.
  • Season — July, or early September.
  • Rhythm — mornings for the water, noon for the stone, evenings for the façade. If the hotel's boat could take me out once, the series would close with a frame of the house seen from the lake.
  • The photographs — as with the Windsor chapter, a gift to the house: yours to use for the website and social media, with a simple credit.

The sea has its chapter. The lake is waiting for its own.
